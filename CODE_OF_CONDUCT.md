# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate respectfully and professionally. We welcome good-faith questions,
bug reports, and differing technical opinions. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

## Enforcement

The initial maintainer investigates reported concerns, may request a change in
behaviour, remove content, limit participation, or ban a participant when
needed to keep the project safe and productive. Decisions are made from the
available evidence and are limited to this project.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately to the initial maintainer using the contact
method published on that maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post reports or
personal information in a public issue. Vulnerabilities follow the separate
[security policy](SECURITY.md).
